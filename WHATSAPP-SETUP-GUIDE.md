## 6. Outreach Script — Electricians in Western Cape

**How to find them:**
1. Facebook Groups: "Electricians Western Cape," "Western Cape Electrical Contractors"
2. Facebook search: "electrician near me" + Cape Town → message their page
3. Google Maps: "electrician Cape Town" → get phone numbers → save to contacts → WhatsApp message

**First message (cold):**
> Hey [Name], saw your work on Facebook/Google. Quick question — do you currently track your job quotes in an app, or still on paper/spreadsheets? I'm with JobOS, we built something specifically for SA tradespeople to create professional quotes and invoices from your phone, even offline. R249/month. Worth a look? https://jobos.pro

**Follow-up (3 days later, no reply):**
> Hey [Name], just following up. No pressure at all — I know admin is the last thing you want to think about. But if you're losing track of quotes or chasing payments, JobOS might save you a few hours a week. Free to try: https://jobos.pro

**If they reply "how does it work?":**
> Simple — you speak into the app, it creates a professional PDF quote. Track all your jobs in a pipeline view. Send invoices via WhatsApp. Never lose another quote. Want a 2-minute demo? I can walk you through it.

**If they say "too expensive":**
> Totally get it. How much is losing one quote worth to you? JobOS is R249/month — less than a tank of petrol. We also have a free tier to try before you commit. No card required.
