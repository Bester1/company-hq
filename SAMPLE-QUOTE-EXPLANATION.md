## What the Sample Quote MUST Include

1. **Header** — Company name + logo placeholder + contact details
2. **Quote # + Date** — Looks official, not ad-hoc
3. **Client info** — "To: Mrs. Peters, 12 Main Road, Cape Town"
4. **Job description** — "Bathroom plumbing repair — leak detection, pipe replacement, tap installation"
5. **Itemized costs** — Labour, materials, subtotal, VAT
6. **Total** — Big, bold, impossible to miss
7. **Terms** — "Valid for 14 days. 50% deposit required."
8. **Signature block** — "Accept Quote: [Signature] Date: ___"
9. **Footer** — "Generated by JobOS — Stop Losing Money to Admin"

This is what separates a professional from a guy texting prices.
