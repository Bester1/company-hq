## 0. Product Reality Check (Correction from v1)

**What JobOS actually IS:**
- Voice-powered business management for tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, painters, builders, handymen)
- Job pipeline tracking, PDF quotes/invoices, Google Calendar sync
- AI smart camera (inventory, document scan, measure)
- Client signatures, offline mode, 8 SA language support
- WhatsApp/email sharing, accounting integration (FNB/Prowash)
- Real competitor: Tradify, Jobber, Housecall Pro — NOT Indeed, SEEK, LinkedIn

**What JobOS IS NOT:**
- A job board / job seeker platform
- Competing with Pnet, CareerJunction, LinkedIn, Monster

**Why this matters:** v1 plan wasted cycles analyzing job boards and pricing for "job posts." The real buyer is the tradesperson running a crew of 1–10 people on WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

| Competitor | Region | Pricing | Weakness | JobOS Advantage |

**Key insight:** The biggest competitor isn't an app — it's doing nothing. SA tradespeople don't buy software because nothing has ever been built for them. JobOS has to convince them this is worth R249/month.

## 3. Marketing Strategy

### Positioning
**Category:** Voice-powered field service management for South African SMEs
**Anti-positioning:** "Not a complicated enterprise system you need a PC for. Not a spreadsheet. Just your phone, your voice, and your jobs."

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Voice = Speed** — "Create a quote while you're on the ladder."
2. **Mobile = Reality** — "Works offline in a basement with no signal."
3. **Local = Trust** — "Speaks your language. Pays in rands."
4. **Money = Motivation** — "Never lose another quote. Get paid faster."

### Channels (Priority Order)

#### 3.1 WhatsApp (Priority #1)
- Create a JobOS WhatsApp Business account
- Build a simple "demo bot" — send "quote" and get a sample PDF quote back
- Share via WhatsApp Status, broadcast lists
- Partner with hardware stores / suppliers to share JobOS QR codes
- **Why:** SA runs on WhatsApp. Every tradesperson lives there.

#### 3.2 TikTok + Instagram Reels (Priority #2)
- Content: "Day in the life of a plumber using JobOS" — 30-second videos
- Show voice commands in action: "Create a quote for Mrs. Peters, plumbing repair, R850"
- Before/after: chaos (lost quotes, late payments) → organized (JobOS pipeline)
- Post daily. Target hashtags: #plumbingSA #electricianSA #builderSA #tradieSA
- **Why:** Visual proof beats text every time. Tradespeople scroll TikTok between jobs.

#### 3.3 Google My Business / Local SEO (Priority #3)
- Create GMB listings for JobOS in major cities
- Blog content: "How to quote for bathroom renovation in Cape Town" (SEO + utility)
- Rank for "quote template South Africa," "invoice app for builders," etc.
- **Why:** When they Google for a solution, JobOS should appear.

#### 3.4 Cold Email / LinkedIn Outreach (Priority #4)
- Target: Construction companies, property managers, franchise owners
- Angle: "Your subcontractors are losing quotes. Here's how to stop it."
- Or direct: "Hey [Name], saw your team on site last week. Do you track job quotes digitally?"
- **Why:** B2B2C play — get construction companies to buy for their subs.

#### 3.5 Facebook Groups (Priority #5)
- Join: "Cape Town Builders Network," "Electricians Western Cape," "Plumbers in SA"
- DON'T spam. Answer questions. When relevant, mention JobOS as the tool you use.
- **Why:** Community trust. Takes time but converts hard.

#### 3.6 YouTube Shorts (Priority #6)
- Repurpose TikTok content
- Longer-form: "How to invoice a client in 2 minutes with JobOS"
- **Why:** Searchable content. "How to create a plumbing quote" ranks forever.

#### 3.7 Referral Program (Priority #7)
- "Invite another tradesperson → both get 1 month free Standard"
- Track via unique referral codes in app
- **Why:** Tradespeople trust other tradespeople. Word of mouth is everything.

### Channels We SKIP (for now)
- LinkedIn ads (wrong audience — white-collar)
- Twitter/X (not where tradespeople are)
- Reddit (SA trades subreddits are tiny)
- Print ads / billboards (too expensive, unmeasurable)
- TV/radio (wrong medium)

## 8. Product Bugs Found (Report to Claude Code Team)

These bugs were identified during codebase review and should be communicated to the Claude Code team before they work on the app:

### Critical
1. **DECLINED jobs vanish from UI**
   - File: src/pages/JobBoard.tsx line ~373
   - Issue: Finished jobs filter only includes PAID || CLOSED. DECLINED jobs disappear — not in "Finished," not in "Lost."
   - Impact: Data integrity. User can't find declined quotes.

2. **Google Calendar events NOT deleted on manual status change**
   - File: src/components/JobModal.tsx, src/pages/JobBoard.tsx
   - Issue: UpdateJobStrategy (voice-only) handles calendar deletion. But if user manually changes status to LOST/CLOSED/DECLINED via UI, deleteCalendarEvent is never called.
   - Impact: Calendar spam. Ghost events persist.

3. **Client signature wiped on JobModal save**
   - File: src/components/JobModal.tsx ~handleSubmit line ~175
   - Issue: clientSignature (the actual image data) is NOT included in the jobData save object. Only clientSignatureDate and clientSignerName are preserved.
   - Impact: Losing signed contracts. Legal exposure.

### Medium
4. **Voice logs incomplete — only tool entries persist**
   - File: voiceEvaluationService.ts → logVoiceEvent()
   - Issue: user and ai speech entries don't write to voice_logs table
   - Impact: Can't audit voice conversations. Fleet learning impaired.

5. **Pending action locks have no TTL**
   - File: system_action_locks table + acquireActionLock()/releaseActionLock()
   - Issue: Lock rows persist forever. If releaseActionLock() fails (exception, early return), the lock stays, causing 20s watchdog timeouts and permanent duplicate blocks.
   - Impact: App becomes unresponsive over time.

6. **Recurring job logic incomplete**
   - File: src/services/db.ts — isRecurring, recurrenceInterval, nextOccurrence exist in types but aren't wired to frontend features
   - Impact: Dead code complexity. Feature gap (no actual recurring jobs UI).

### Recommendation
Ask Claude Code to:
1. Fix #1–#3 before any new features (user-facing bugs)
2. Audit #5 (#4–#6 are lower priority)
3. Add unit tests for calendar deletion and signature persistence
