## Why TikTok + Instagram for Tradespeople?

The old assumption: tradespeople aren't on social media. Wrong.

- SA has **25M+ TikTok users**. Plumbers and electricians scroll between jobs.
- Instagram Reels = TikTok's twin. Same content, cross-posted.
- TikTok algorithm is **meritocratic** — a plumber with 50 followers can get 10K views if the content hits.
- Visual proof works: showing a voice command creating a quote in 10 seconds beats any sales pitch.
- Your competitors (Tradify, Jobber) are NOT here. You own this space.

**The goal:** Become the "that app from TikTok" that tradespeople recognize.

## 2. Content Strategy — The 5 Video Types

Post **1 video per day minimum** on both platforms. Same video, cross-posted. Takes 10 minutes.

### Type 1: The "Day in the Life" (40% of content)
**Format:** POV camera. Follow a tradesperson through their day.

**Script structure:**
- 0:00–0:03 — Hook: "6AM. Plumber. Monday."
- 0:03–0:08 — Problem: "Got 4 jobs today. No system. Just my phone and a notepad."
- 0:08–0:18 — App demo: [screen recording of voice command creating a job] "Speak the job in. Quote auto-generated. Sent to client via WhatsApp."
- 0:18–0:25 — Result: "All 4 jobs tracked. Invoices sent. Payment coming in. Done by 4PM."
- 0:25–0:30 — CTA: "Stop losing money to admin. Link in bio."

**Why it works:** Tradespeople see themselves. It's not an ad — it's a mirror.

---

### Type 2: The "Before/After" (25% of content)
**Format:** Split screen or quick cuts.

**Script structure:**
- 0:00–0:05 — Before: messy notebook, lost quote, angry client text "Where's my invoice?"
- 0:05–0:15 — After: clean JobOS pipeline, professional PDF quote, "Invoice paid — thank you!"
- 0:15–0:20 — CTA: "Your admin doesn't have to be chaos. JobOS. Link in bio."

**Why it works:** Pain → solution. The before MUST be relatable.

---

### Type 3: The "Quick Demo" (20% of content)
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover.

**Script structure:**
- 0:00–0:03 — Hook: "Create a professional quote in 10 seconds. Watch."
- 0:03–0:12 — Demo: [screen recording] Voice input → quote generated → PDF preview
- 0:12–0:18 — Feature callout: "Works offline. Sends via WhatsApp. Tracks in pipeline."
- 0:18–0:25 — CTA: "R249/month. Less than a tank of petrol. Link in bio."

**Why it works:** Pure proof. No fluff. Skeptical tradespeople need to SEE it work.

---

### Type 4: The "Myth Bust" (10% of content)
**Format:** Talking head or text on screen.

**Script structure:**
- 0:00–0:03 — Hook: "You think admin software is expensive? Wrong."
- 0:03–0:10 — Myth: "ServiceTitan costs R5,000+/month. Needs a PC. Training for days."
- 0:10–0:20 — Truth: "JobOS is R249/month. On your phone. Set up in 60 seconds."
- 0:20–0:25 — CTA: "Stop overpaying. Link in bio."

**Why it works:** Tradespeople think software = expensive + complicated. You break that.

---

### Type 5: The "Client Story" (5% of content, when available)
**Format:** Interview or selfie video with a real user.

**Script structure:**
- 0:00–0:05 — Intro: "Meet John. Electrician. Bellville. 15 years in the trade."
- 0:05–0:15 — Problem: "I was losing 2 quotes a week. Forgot to invoice a R4,000 job."
- 0:15–0:25 — Solution: "JobOS tracks everything. I speak the job in, quote goes out, payment comes in."
- 0:25–0:30 — CTA: "Join John. Link in bio."

**Why it works:** Social proof from someone who looks like them. Nothing converts harder.

## 5. Posting Cadence

**TikTok:**
- Post: 6:30–8:00 AM (tradespeople waking up, checking phone before work)
- Alternative: 12:00–1:00 PM (lunch break scroll)
- Post EVERY DAY. Missing a day kills momentum.

**Instagram Reels:**
- Cross-post same video within 30 minutes of TikTok
- Instagram favors Reels posted 7–9 AM and 7–9 PM
- Add to Stories daily (behind-the-scenes, polls, "Should I film X or Y?")

**Stories (Instagram only, daily):**
- Poll: "Paper quotes or digital quotes?"
- Quiz: "How many quotes did you lose this month?"
- Countdown: "New feature dropping in 3 days"

## 7. Bio Link Strategy

**Option 1 — Direct to jobos.pro (simplest)**
- Pros: No friction, single CTA
- Cons: Can't track which platform converted

**Option 2 — Linktree or similar**
- Links: Free trial | Pricing | Demo video | WhatsApp Business
- Pros: Multiple CTAs, analytics
- Cons: One extra click

**Recommendation:** Start with direct jobos.pro link. Switch to Linktree when you have 1K+ followers and want to segment traffic.

**If you're underperforming:**

- < 10 comments/post → not asking questions. End every video with "What do you think?" or "Comment YES if this is you."
- < 50 link clicks → CTA isn't clear. Say "Link in bio" OUT LOUD in every video.
